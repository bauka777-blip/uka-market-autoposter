Privacy Policy

Uka Market AutoPoster

Last updated: October 4, 2026

1. About the Service

Uka Market AutoPoster is a content automation tool that helps prepare product videos and upload them to a connected TikTok account for further manual editing and publication.

2. TikTok Data

The service may use TikTok Login Kit and the TikTok Content Posting API. After authorization, the service may access basic account information provided by TikTok and upload video content to the authorized TikTok account.

3. How Data Is Used

TikTok authorization data is used only to provide the requested integration, including account authorization and video upload functionality.

4. Data Sharing

The service does not sell personal information. Data is not shared with third parties except when necessary to provide the service or comply with applicable law.

5. Data Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect authorization credentials and other information used by the service.

6. User Control

Users may revoke the application's access through their TikTok account settings. Authorization data may also be removed when access is revoked or when it is no longer required for the service.

7. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the service or its functionality changes. The current version will be published on this page.
