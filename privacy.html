Uka Market AutoPoster

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Uka Market AutoPoster (the “Service”) processes information when preparing product videos and uploading them to an authorized TikTok account.

1. Information processed

  • Content: product information and media supplied for video preparation, and the resulting video files.
  • TikTok authorization: OAuth authorization credentials, including access tokens and refresh tokens when issued, and account identifiers needed to associate authorization with the intended account.
  • Upload information: API responses, upload identifiers, processing status, and error information needed to complete or troubleshoot uploads.

The TikTok integration uses the video.upload permission. It does not require your TikTok password. This permission is used to upload prepared videos for manual editing and publication; it is not permission to automatically publish videos.

2. How information is used

Information is processed to prepare product videos, maintain the authorized TikTok connection, send videos to the correct account, and handle upload results or errors. The Service does not use Direct Post. You decide whether to publish uploaded content through TikTok.

3. Transfer to TikTok and sharing

Prepared video files and the authorization and request information necessary for an upload are sent to TikTok through its Content Posting API. TikTok processes information under its own Privacy Policy. The Service does not sell personal information. Information is otherwise shared only as necessary to operate the Service or comply with applicable law.

4. Retention and security

Content, authorization credentials, and upload records are retained only for as long as needed to provide the Service, resolve operational issues, or meet applicable legal obligations. The retention period depends on the type of information and its purpose.

Authorization credentials and other non-public information should be handled as confidential information. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your choices and deletion requests

You can stop using the Service and revoke its access through your TikTok account settings. Revoking access prevents further authorized uploads but does not itself delete videos or information already transferred to TikTok. Manage that content through TikTok.

To request access to or deletion of information held by the Service, contact the repository owner through the official GitHub repository and request a private contact channel. Do not post tokens, video files, or other personal information publicly. Requests may require verification of your connection to the account; some records may need to be retained to comply with applicable law.

6. Changes and contact

Updates to this policy will be published on this page with a revised date. Privacy questions can be directed to the repository owner using the contact route above.
