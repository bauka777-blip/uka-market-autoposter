Uka Market AutoPoster

Content preparation and TikTok upload automation tool.

About the service

Uka Market AutoPoster helps prepare product videos and upload them to the connected TikTok account for further manual editing and publication.

Videos are not automatically published. The user reviews and publishes the uploaded content through TikTok.

TikTok Integration

The service uses TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API. Authorized users can send prepared video content to their TikTok account for further editing and manual publication.
